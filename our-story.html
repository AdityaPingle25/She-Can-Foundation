Our Core Purpose

Our Mission:
At She Can Foundation, our mission is to empower underprivileged women across India by providing access to education, healthcare, and economic opportunities. We strive to create a world where every woman is equipped with the tools and confidence she needs to overcome challenges and reach her full potential.

Our Vision:
Our vision is a world where every woman has equal access to education, healthcare, and economic opportunities. We believe that empowering women is not just a matter of justice, but a critical component in building a better, more equitable society. Our goal is to create a society that celebrates women's contributions and provides them with the opportunity to thrive.

Our History

The Journey of Impact

2021

Our Inception

Started inside a single room in New Delhi by Reeta Mishra and active local volunteers. Our early operations focused on distributing basic menstrual health supplies to 500+ girls in rural Delhi villages.

2023

Sanitation Awareness Drives

Launched comprehensive menstrual health campaigns across 100+ villages. Successfully distributed over 50,000 dignity kits and conducted hygiene workshops in municipal primary classrooms.

2025

National Society Status

Registered officially under the Indian Society Act of 1860. Expanded operations to 250+ villages, empowering 1,20,000+ girls and women with direct pads supply, skill workshops, and volunteer networks.

A Message from Our President

Dear Friends,

When we first laid the bricks for She Can Foundation in 2021, we were driven by a single, heart-wrenching realization: that thousands of young girls were dropping out of school simply because they could not afford basic menstrual hygiene products. Menstruation, a natural symbol of life and health, had become a source of silent shame, health risks, and lost education.

We set out to change this story. What began as a handful of passionate volunteers distributing sanitation supplies in a single village has blossomed into a full-scale movement. We are proud to have supported over 1,20,000+ girls and women across 250+ villages in India.

But our journey is far from over. For every girl we keep in school, we are securing a future leader, a mother who will thrive, and a community transformed. Our conviction remains absolute: when you empower a woman, you empower a nation.

Thank you for your trust, your kindness, and for believing in our mission. Together, she will rise, and she will thrive.

Reeta Mishra
Founder & President
Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

The foundational elements that keep us true to our mission and transparent to our community.

Compassion

We serve with empathy and care. Compassion is the heartbeat of our organization, guiding our projects and community interventions with genuine warmth.

Equality

We believe in absolute equal opportunities. Every female should have unimpeded access to sanitation, quality education, and career opportunities.

Integrity

Honesty, transparency, and accountability guide our operations. We maintain high standards in allocating donations, field reports, and corporate governance.

"Compassion, equality, and integrity are the cornerstones of She Can Foundation's work. These values drive us in everything we do and help us stay true to our mission of empowering women. We believe in community-led development, where local insights and ideas drive transformative change. Transparency and accountability are the pillars of our work, as we work to build a brighter future for all."
