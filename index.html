About Us

Global Vision,
Local Action

She Can Foundation is a government-registered non-governmental organization dedicated to empowering women and creating a more equitable society across India and beyond.

We provide support, resources, sanitary hygiene kits, and livelihood training to women in communities — working closely with local stakeholders, groups, and volunteers to ensure our programs are effective, sustainable, and highly transparent.

📚

Education

Providing learning opportunities and skill-building for every girl.

🏥

Health & Safety

Supporting women's health and menstrual hygiene awareness drives.

💼

Livelihood

Enabling financial independence through craft training and resources.

📣

Advocacy

Raising awareness and challenging barriers faced by women everywhere.
